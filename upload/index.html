Questions

Before you sign up

Still unsure? Talk to the team — we answer within a day.

Is estate hub free?

Search, listings, and neighborhood scores are free forever. Pro ($19/month) unlocks the full 180-metric report, unlimited saved searches, and price-drop alerts across every market you track.

Where does the data come from?

Licensed MLS feeds, county assessor and deed records, census and school district data, and our own rent index built from 4.1 million active rental listings. Every metric on a listing page cites its source and refresh date.

How often is it refreshed?

Listing status and price changes sync every 15 minutes. Derived analytics — yield, price index, neighborhood scores — recompute nightly across all 340 metros.

Do you sell my search history?

No. We do not sell or share personal data with agents, lenders, or advertisers. You will never get a cold call because you saved a listing here.

Can I use it as an agent or investor?

Yes. Teams gets you shared saved searches, client-ready PDF reports with your own branding, and an API for pulling the index into your own models.
